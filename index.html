Whack-a-mole: a reflex game. Moles pop out of a grid of nine holes. Whack a popped-up mole by clicking or tapping it, or move between holes with the arrow keys and whack with Enter or Space. Click, tap, or press Enter or Space to start a round; when the sixty-second round ends, click, tap, or press Enter or Space to play again. Each mole you whack scores one point.
